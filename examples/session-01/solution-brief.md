# Solution brief: dispatch and delivery coordination

FruitTrucks distributes fresh fruit and vegetables from its depots in Valencia and Madrid to supermarkets, independent grocers, restaurants and hotels. Every morning around 110 refrigerated trucks and vans leave the depots.

Dispatchers assign vehicles and drivers to routes before departure. During the day some routes must be changed: a vehicle breaks down, a customer adds a late order, or a driver falls ill. Today these route changes are made by phone, and drivers sometimes miss them.

Customer service agents are often asked when a delivery will arrive. They cannot answer without calling the driver.

FruitTrucks wants an application that allows dispatchers to reassign routes during the day and notify drivers of changes, and that lets customers know when their delivery will arrive.

The application must use the existing ERP, fleet management and HR systems. It must not replace them.

Drivers carry a company phone. Some deliveries are in rural areas with poor mobile coverage.

Goods are perishable and must stay refrigerated.
